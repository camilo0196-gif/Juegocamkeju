A humanoid SkeletonProfile preset. Description  A SkeletonProfile as a preset that is optimized for the human form. This exists for standardization, so all parameters are read-only. A humanoid skeleton profile contains 56 bones divided into 4 groups: "Body" , "Face" , "LeftHand" , and "RightHand" .
